GitHub Pages hosts this site and records visitor IP addresses for security. [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)

The site uses Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google your IP address when it requests a font. [Google Fonts and privacy](https://fonts.googleblog.com/2022/11/your-privacy-and-google-fonts.html)
